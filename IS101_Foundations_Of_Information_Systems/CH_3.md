
# Cyber Security Summary

## 1. Internet Growth
- **Key Idea:** The number of internet users has significantly increased from 2005 to 2022, reaching 5.4 billion users. This highlights the increasing reliance on the internet and the growing potential security risks.

## 2. Why Information Security?
- **Key Points:** 
  - The number of internet users is rapidly increasing.
  - Cybersecurity losses are projected to reach $10.5 trillion by 2025, emphasizing the need for robust information security measures.

## 3. Cybersecurity 2023 Roundup
- **Statistics:** The average cost of a data breach in 2023 is $4.45 million, with 2.6 billion personal records stolen in 2021 and 2022 alone. Cybercrime is expected to cost $8 trillion in 2023.

## 4. Cybersecurity vs. Information Security
- **Key Difference:**
  - **InfoSec** covers all types of information protection (digital, physical, intellectual) while focusing on confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA triad).
  - **Cybersecurity** focuses specifically on protecting digital systems and data from cyberattacks.

## 5. Cybersecurity Overview
- **Goal:** Cybersecurity protects systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, damage, and theft, dealing with digital threats from cybercriminals, hackers, or user errors.

## 6. Information Security Triad
- **CIA Triad:**
  - **Confidentiality:** Ensures only authorized access.
  - **Integrity:** Ensures data is accurate and unaltered.
  - **Availability:** Ensures data and systems are accessible when needed.

## 7. Authentication & Authorization
- **Authentication:** Verifies the identity of a user or system.
- **Authorization:** Determines the actions a verified user or system is allowed to perform.
- **Examples:** Single-factor, two-factor, and multi-factor authentication methods.

## 8. Non-repudiation and Auditability
- **Non-repudiation:** Ensures actions cannot be denied after they are performed (e.g., using digital signatures).
- **Auditability:** Ensures that actions within a system can be traced and reviewed (e.g., audit logs and trails).

## 9. Third-party Protection
- **Goal:** Secures interactions between organizations and third-party vendors, ensuring data security through contracts, audits, and compliance checks.

## 10. Cybersecurity Goals Summary
- **Key Goals:** Authentication, Authorization, Non-repudiation, Auditability, and Third-party Protection are fundamental cybersecurity goals aimed at ensuring security, accountability, and transparency.

## 11. Importance of Cybersecurity
- **Reasons:** Data protection, business continuity, preventing financial loss, preserving reputation, and legal compliance are critical for organizations.

## 12. Types of Cybersecurity Threats
- **Examples:** Malware, Phishing, Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks, SQL Injection, and Zero-Day Exploits are common threats.

## 13. Key Concepts in Cybersecurity
- **Examples:** Attack surface, vulnerability, threat, risk, exploit, and defense-in-depth strategies are key components to understand when securing a system.

## 14. Cybersecurity Principles
- **Principles:** Least Privilege, Separation of Duties, Defense in Depth, Zero Trust, and Security by Design are essential principles for a secure environment.

## 15. Key Roles in Cybersecurity
- **Roles:** Security Analyst, Penetration Tester, Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Incident Responder, and Security Engineer play vital roles in protecting digital assets.

## 16. Cybersecurity Lifecycle
- **Lifecycle:** Identify risks, implement measures, monitor for threats, respond to incidents, and restore normal operations post-incident.

## 17. Emerging Trends in Cybersecurity
- **Trends:** AI/ML for threat detection, Cloud Security, IoT Security, Blockchain for security, and Quantum Computing represent the future of cybersecurity.

## 18. Best Practices for Cybersecurity
- **Practices:** Strong passwords, two-factor authentication (2FA), software updates, data backup, user education, and firewalls/intrusion detection systems are vital for securing systems.

## 19. Cybersecurity Jobs
- **Career Paths:** Various cybersecurity roles include Penetration Testers, Incident Response Specialists, Network Security Engineers, and Cloud Security Specialists, reflecting the diverse career opportunities in the field.
