# IT Infrastructure and Emerging Technologies

## 1. IT Infrastructure
- Defined as the set of physical devices, software, and services needed to run an enterprise.
- Includes:
  - Computing platforms (servers, PCs, mobile devices)
  - Telecommunications services
  - Data management systems
  - Application software
  - Physical facilities management
  - IT management and education
- Seen as a "service platform" that shows the true value of IT investments.

---

## 2. Evolution of IT Infrastructure
- **Mainframe and Minicomputer Era (1959–present)**  
  Large centralized computing with IBM mainframes and DEC minicomputers.
- **Personal Computer Era (1981–present)**  
  Growth of desktop computing with IBM PC and rise of personal productivity software.
- **Client/Server Era (1983–present)**  
  Processing split between servers and client machines; multi-tier architectures appear.
- **Enterprise Computing Era (1992–present)**  
  Integration of disparate networks and systems using Internet standards.
- **Cloud and Mobile Computing Era (2000–present)**  
  Internet-based services, virtual resources, and rapid growth of mobile devices.

---

## 3. Technology Drivers of Infrastructure Evolution
- **Moore’s Law**: Computing power doubles roughly every 18 months, reducing costs.  
- **Law of Mass Digital Storage**: Storage capacity doubles while costs decline sharply.  
- **Metcalfe’s Law**: Network value increases with more users, boosting demand.  
- **Declining Communication Costs**: Internet access costs dropped, fueling global connectivity.

---

## 4. Components of IT Infrastructure
1. **Computer Hardware Platforms**: PCs, laptops, smartphones, servers, mainframes.
2. **Operating Systems**: Windows, Linux, Unix, iOS, Android, Chrome OS.
3. **Enterprise Applications**: ERP and business applications (SAP, Oracle).
4. **Data Management & Storage**: Databases (Oracle, MySQL, SQL Server) and SANs.
5. **Networking/Telecom**: Telecom services, routers, switches (Cisco, Juniper).
6. **Internet Platforms**: Web hosting, intranets, extranets, development tools.
7. **Consulting & System Integration**: Integration of new systems with legacy systems (IBM, Accenture).

---

## 5. Current Trends in Hardware Platforms
- **Grid Computing**: Connects remote computers into a virtual supercomputer.  
- **Virtualization**: Runs multiple OS instances on one machine, cutting costs.  
- **Cloud Computing**: Provides computing, storage, and apps as virtual resources.  
  - **IaaS**: Infrastructure as a Service (Amazon EC2, Google Compute Engine).  
  - **PaaS**: Platform as a Service (Google App Engine, IBM tools).  
  - **SaaS**: Software as a Service (Google Apps, Salesforce).  
- **Public, Private, and Hybrid Clouds**: Different deployment models with varying control and cost.

---

## 6. Current Trends in Software Platforms
- **Open-Source Software**: Free, modifiable tools (Linux, Apache, Firefox).  
- **Linux OS**: Widely used in servers, mobile devices, and high-performance computing.  
- **Software Outsourcing**: Hiring external firms for software development.  
- **Cloud-Based Software Services**: Accessing applications via the Internet under Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
